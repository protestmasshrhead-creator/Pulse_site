Legal information

These documents apply to the whole Pulse Call website, in every country section and language version. They are written to meet the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, and the EU GDPR where we recruit in the European Union. Where the two regimes differ, we apply the stricter of them to everyone.

Questions about your data

Write to hr@pulse-call.com. We answer requests about personal data within one month, free of charge, and no particular form of words is needed.

Complaints about how we handle personal data

Email hr@pulse-call.com with "Data protection complaint" in the subject line, or write to the address below. We acknowledge the complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome without undue delay — the procedure required by section 103 of the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, which we apply to everyone. You do not have to come to us first: you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom, or to the authority of your country if you are in the EU, at any time. The full procedure is in the Privacy Policy.
