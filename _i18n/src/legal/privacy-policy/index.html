Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Pulse Call collects through this website, why we need it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It applies to every page and every language version of the site.

In short

We collect only what you give us in the application form, plus minimal technical data needed to run the site. We use it to consider you for a role. We do not sell your data or pass it on for third-party advertising. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to hr@pulse-call.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers personal data processed through pulse-call.com, including all country sections and language versions, and any application you submit through the site. It does not cover:

  • third-party websites we link to, which have their own policies;
  • processing of employee data once you have been hired, which is covered by separate employment documentation;
  • communications you start with us through channels outside the site, although the same principles apply.

Detailed internal rules on how we handle applicant data are set out in our Personal Data Processing Policy.

5. Referral programme

Under our "refer a friend" programme we receive contact details of a person from someone else rather than from that person directly. Additional rules therefore apply.

If you refer someone

By giving us another person's telephone number you confirm that you have asked them first and that they agreed to be contacted. You are responsible if that permission was not obtained.

If someone referred you

When we first contact you we will tell you, as required by Article 14 UK GDPR / EU GDPR:

  • who we are and how to contact us;
  • that we obtained your number through a referral, and from whom if you ask;
  • why we are calling and on what lawful basis;
  • where to read this policy.

If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so promptly and will not contact you again.

8. International transfers

We operate across more than ten countries, so data may move between the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and other countries. Where data leaves the UK or the EEA, we rely on one of the following safeguards:

  • an adequacy decision covering the destination country — the UK and the EU each recognise the other as providing adequate protection, subject to those decisions remaining in force;
  • the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum, together with a transfer risk assessment;
  • another appropriate safeguard permitted by Chapter V of the UK GDPR or EU GDPR.

You can request a copy of the safeguards we rely on by writing to hr@pulse-call.com.

11. How to exercise your rights

Write to hr@pulse-call.com and tell us which right you want to use. No special form is needed.

  • We respond within one month of receiving your request.
  • For complex requests we may extend this by up to two further months, telling you why within the first month.
  • There is no charge. A fee, or refusal, is possible only where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.
  • We may ask you to confirm your identity if we have genuine doubts about who is making the request.

12. Complaints

13. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, as required by Article 32:

  • data transmitted over HTTPS;
  • access control — data is visible only to staff who need it for their work;
  • confidentiality obligations for everyone with access;
  • written data processing agreements with all processors;
  • periodic review of access rights.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required, and we tell you directly where the risk is high.

14. Children

This site is intended for adult applicants. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an application came from a minor, we delete the data.

15. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about your application by automated means alone, and we do not carry out profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Article 22. Decisions to invite a candidate to interview and to hire are made by people.

16. Changes to this policy

The current version is always available at this address, with the version number and date shown at the top. If we make a material change, we will notify you through the contact details you gave us or place a prominent notice on the site.
