About

A company, not a one-off side gig

Pulse Call is an international call centre. We hire operators across Europe and work with them for the long run: we train from scratch, provide the base and the scripts, and pay every 10 days.

What we do

Conversation is our core product

We work with companies that need to talk to their clients by voice: to advise, to process orders, to carry enquiries through to a result. Our operators call from a ready base and manage deals in the CRM.

The products are simple consumer goods, including beauty products. All of them are legal and certified. This is a matter of principle: an operator is accountable for what they tell a client and must be confident in what they are offering.

What the job does not involve. No cold base "off the street", no hunting for clients, and no products you would be uncomfortable describing to your family.
Why people work with us

The reasons operators name themselves

Predictable moneyThe payout cycle is every 10 days, with no "wait until the end of the month". Income consists of an hourly rate plus a percentage of sales.
Entry without experienceTraining from scratch takes 3 to 7 days. €150 is paid for completed training after your first month of work.
Support, not solitudeA mentor on your first calls, a product trainer and a shift supervisor. You can ask questions as you go.
A schedule, not chaosThe main pattern is 2/2. Switching to 5/2 is possible by agreement with your manager.
Work from anywhereYou need a laptop or computer and a stable internet connection. Moving between countries does not get in the way.
