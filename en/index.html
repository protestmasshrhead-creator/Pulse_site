Why join us

Four reasons, not vague promises

There are plenty of remote work ads, and almost all of them promise the same thing. We answer the questions that usually go unanswered.

  • Money arrives every 10 days, not "at the end of the month". Your first payout comes before your first month is even over.
  • No need to look for clients. The base, the scripts and the CRM come from the company. You focus on the conversation, not on hunting for contacts.
  • Sales experience is not required. Training takes 3 to 7 days, and you get paid €150 for it after your first month of work.
  • You are not left alone after training. A mentor is beside you on your first calls, and a supervisor is responsible for the shift.
